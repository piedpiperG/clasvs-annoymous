# CLASVS: Anonymous Audio Demonstrations

Supplementary demonstrations for **Continuous-Latent Autoregression for Melody-Preserving Lyric Editing in Singing Voice Synthesis**.

Open **[the interactive demo](./index.html)** for three real-song edits and eight controlled comparisons spanning partial substitution, full substitution, deletion, and insertion.

All three real-song audio versions start at the first vocal line. Instrumental introductions are removed, and displayed lyric times refer to the trimmed audio. Intervening musical passages are preserved.

The method uses persistent lyric and melody controls, global semantic feedback, and local acoustic transitions. Training uses content-consistent reconstruction without paired counterfactual edits.

Audio excerpts are provided for non-commercial academic comparison. Generated samples contain synthetic singing and altered lyrics. Rights in underlying music remain with their respective rights holders.
